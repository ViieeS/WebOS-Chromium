Chromium for your LG TV

Chromium with the full Chrome interface, running natively on webOS: tabs, address bar, menus, bookmarks, history, downloads and extensions from the Chrome Web Store. No root needed.

What you get

  • Built for the TV's own processor, display server and GPU driver. No emulation.
  • Pages drawn on the TV's GPU.
  • A sharp 1920x1080 window with the interface at 1.5x.
  • Hardware video decoding on every site, up to 1080p60.
  • The remote's arrows, OK and Back move through pages, menus and tabs. The Magic Remote pointer works as a mouse, and typing uses LG's on-screen keyboard.
  • TV Tools, built in on the toolbar: a video quality limit for any site, TV versions of websites, lite mode, a tab limit and unloading of background tabs.
  • Bookmarks and settings are kept on the TV and survive reboots.

Which TVs

webOS 4.4 (2018-2019 models) and newer. Check Settings > Support > TV Information > webOS TV Version. On older TVs the app shows a message and closes.

Good to know

  • Testers have run it on webOS 4.10, 5.5, 5.6, 6.5, 7.6, 8 and 10. How fast it is depends on the model.
  • No Netflix or other DRM video, and no Google account sync.
