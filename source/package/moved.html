Chromium has moved

This app, Chromium (old), gets no more updates. New versions come as a new app, Chromium Browser (id io.github.sarahahzub.chromium), in Homebrew Channel.

  1. Open Homebrew Channel and install Chromium Browser.
  2. Open Chromium Browser. Your bookmarks and history come over by themselves, even if you opened it before.
  3. Remove Chromium (old). It keeps working until you do.

Saved passwords and site logins don't move, so sign in again in the new app.

Until you remove this app, a copy of your bookmarks and history waits in its folder, where other apps on the TV can read it.
